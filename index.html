About Me

I'm a cosplayer, photographer, and prop maker. I'm from DC so I'll frequently attend cons in that area but I"m currently based out of Pittsburgh so I mostly attend events in that area.

Currently I make props between con seasons, take commissions for props to be delivered by con deadlines, and open myself for photography bookings at any cons that I attend.

I consider myself an intermediate photographer, and always enjoy a challenging photo concept or editing challenge.

Feel free to explore some snippets of the events I've attended and my thoughts on how I strive to blend my passions for all things nerdy with my love for photography.

Cosplay Photography

I've been cosplaying since 2016, and I've been an active part of both the physical and online anime communities for years. Over time, I've come to know a lot about different characters, fandoms, and medias which i believe is an important part of making props or doing photoshoots, so I'll be sure to research before accepting a commission.

Portrait Photography

My journey in photography began in 2018 when I started capturing moments at graduations, concerts, birthdays, events, and spontaneous group gatherings. I've also ventured into candid photography during photo walks. As of now I do scheduled shoots, corproate events, and I continue to do graduation photos.
